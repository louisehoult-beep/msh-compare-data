Conferences for nurses working in industry

The meetings where clinical educators, clinical specialists and industry nurses meet the NHS and each other: the National Association of Medical Device Educators and Trainers (NAMDET), the Association of British HealthTech Industries (ABHI), the Association of the British Pharmaceutical Industry (ABPI) and the clinical societies behind them. Free to join, and free Medical Sales Hub access comes with it. Dates and venues are as published by each organiser, checked 26/10/2026.
