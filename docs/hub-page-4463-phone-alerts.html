Phone alerts for new news

Alerts at 9am, 12 noon and 4pm, only when something new has gone live for the specialities you pick. Tap it to see the headlines, each linking to its source, with a link to that speciality's page here on the Hub. No app to install and no email: your phone is the subscriber, and you can turn it off any time.

Open the alerts page from the button above in Safari or Chrome, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then open the new Hub alerts icon and turn alerts on there.
