Live Desk app: privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

The Live Desk app is run by Elevate and Thrive Ltd (company number 17154474), 2 Lakeside, Sturton By Stow, Lincoln, LN1 2TZ. We are the data controller for the information below. Contact: contact@elevateandthrive.uk.

What we hold and why

  • Your email address, to create your account and send your sign-in codes.
  • Your Hub member number, only if you connect a Medical Sales Intelligence Hub membership, to confirm your access.
  • Your subscription status (product, renewal or end date), if you subscribe in the app. Apple or Google take the payment; we never see your card details.
  • Your phone's notification token and the specialities you pick, only if you turn alerts on, to send you alerts.

The lawful basis is contract: we need this information to provide the app you signed up for. We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and we don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: account, sign-in and app data storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering alerts to your phone (through Apple's push service on iPhone).
  • RevenueCat: checking in-app subscription status with Apple and Google.
  • Apple and Google: in-app payments, under their own privacy policies.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can do that at any time in the app: Account, then Delete my account. That removes your account, Hub connection, subscription record and alert settings straight away. Turning alerts off removes your phone's notification token. A store subscription has to be cancelled separately in your Apple or Google account.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email contact@elevateandthrive.uk. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
