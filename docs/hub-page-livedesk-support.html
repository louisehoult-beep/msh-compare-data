Live Desk app: help

Signing in

Enter your email and we'll send you a 6-digit code. If it doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check your junk folder, then ask for a new code.

Hub members

Tap Connect my Hub membership. The Hub opens; log in if asked, and the app opens again, connected. The app re-checks your membership about once a month with a single tap.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. To cancel or change yours, use your App Store or Google Play account settings. On a new phone, sign in and tap Restore purchases.

Alerts

Alerts come at 9am, 12 noon and 4pm, only when something new lands in your specialities. Turn them on in the Alerts tab. If nothing arrives, check notifications are allowed for Live Desk in your phone's settings.
