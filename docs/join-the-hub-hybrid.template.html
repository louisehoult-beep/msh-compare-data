What changes about your week

Four things the Hub does for your number

Think about the week ahead of you. These are the four things the Hub changes about it.

01 · This week

Know which accounts to call, and why now

Open tenders, contract awards and frameworks coming up to expiry, filtered to your speciality. They tell you where a decision is being made, so the call has a reason.

Inside: Frameworks, tenders and awards and the Sales Triggers Desk

The Live Desk

Sales trigger example: out of stock. Contact procurement and offer your help. They will likely be looking for a replacement, even a temporary one, and it builds relationships and trust.

02 · Before you walk in

Carry the line the buyer cannot argue with

Every item is read on the issuing organisation's own page and written up with the angle to take. Help Me Prepare turns company, trust, speciality and audience into a brief for that meeting.

Inside: Help Me Prepare and the Stakeholder Mapper

03 · On the framework

Beat the competitor who is already listed

See the frameworks they hold, when those end and who else sits on them. Compare Your Product puts yours beside theirs, in the same category, on the manufacturers' own pages and the NHS Supply Chain catalogue.

Inside: Company Intelligence Reports and Compare Your Product

04 · Never blindsided

Hear about the recall before your customer does

MHRA alerts and suspended or discontinued NHS Supply Chain lines, with the supplier behind each brand and the listed alternatives. The Supply Disruption Tracker is where a gap in a competitor's range becomes your call.

The Value Case Calculator

Build the value case the finance director will sign

Clinical benefit gets you the meeting. A saving the trust can check gets you the order. The calculator turns the problem your product solves into an annual figure for that trust, with the assumptions shown so a buyer can test each one.

  • Start from the problem your product solves, not your list price
  • Get a conservative annual saving you can defend in front of procurement and finance
  • Add the carbon saving against a competitor product for the sustainability question
  • Send the result straight into your meeting brief

Questions before you decide

Straight answers

Is this a contact list?

No. You get job titles, the questions to ask and links to go and identify people yourself. Nobody's personal details are stored or sold.

Is it just a news feed?

No. Every item is read on the issuing organisation's own page, checked, and written up with the line you can actually use. Alongside it are the tools, the company reports and the Value Case Calculator.

How often is the Rep's Briefing sent?

Once a week, by email, on Monday morning. The desks inside the Hub update in between.

What if my company already buys market data?

This is the working layer underneath it: what changed this week, who decides at that trust, and what to say. It is built for the rep, not the boardroom.

Can I expense it?

The annual plans are a single payment with one invoice, which is usually the easier route through a team budget. For more than one person, see team access above.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly access runs while your subscription is active and you can cancel any time. Annual access runs for the 12 months you have paid for.
