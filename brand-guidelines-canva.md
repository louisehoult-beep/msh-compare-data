CMYK values are screen-to-CMYK maths, not a print profile. Give the hex to your printer for a proper match.

## 1. Brand essence

* Name: Medical Sales Intelligence Hub (short form: the Hub)
* Audience: medical sales professionals and people moving into medical sales
* Personality: credible, calm, practical, warm. Expert without being stiff.

### Usage ratio

* Navy and cream carry most layouts: roughly 70%.
* Gold is the accent: roughly 10%. One gold element per layout is enough.
* Status colours only for status. Never as decoration.

## 3. Colour pairings

* Navy background: pale blue (#DBE3EE) or white text, light gold (#E0BE8E) accents.
* Cream or white background: ink (#132238) text, gold (#A8842C) accents, navy headings.
* Do not set gold text smaller than 18px on cream. Use dark gold (#8C6A1C) for small text.
* Do not put gold text on mid navy at small sizes.

## 4. Typography

* Primary: Inter (headings and body). Available free in Canva.
* Headings: Inter ExtraBold (800), uppercase for the wordmark with 1.6px letter-spacing.
* Subheadings: Inter SemiBold (600).
* Body: Inter Regular (400), 1.6 to 1.75 line height.
* Editorial or long-read accent: Source Serif 4 (fallback Georgia). Use sparingly.
* Fallback stack: -apple-system, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif.

## 5. Logo and wordmark

* Wordmark: bold uppercase Inter, wide letter-spacing, navy on light, white on navy.
* Marker: small gold (#A8842C) diamond (a square rotated 45 degrees) before the wordmark.
* Logo file, clear space, minimum size and misuse rules: To confirm. Send the logo file and I'll complete this section.

## 6. Imagery

* Real UK clinical and commercial settings. Nobody wears a tie in UK hospital images.
* Navy overlay (about 55 to 96% opacity) on photo backgrounds so text stays legible.
* Avoid stock-photo clichés (handshakes, stethoscopes on laptops).

## 7. Layout

* Generous white space. Thin borders (#E6E0D4) rather than heavy boxes.
* Rounded corners about 8px on buttons and cards.
* Primary button: navy fill, white text. Secondary: white fill, navy border and text.
* Links: mid navy (#14304F), bold, underlined in gold.

## 8. Voice

* Warm, plain English, UK spelling. Confident without overstating.
* Contractions. No jargon without a reason. No em dashes or en dashes.
* Detailed voice rules: To confirm.

## 9. Setting this up in Canva

1. Canva, then Brand Kit, then Colours: add each hex above to a "Hub Core" palette (and "Hub Supporting" for the rest).
2. Fonts: set Inter as heading, subheading and body. Add Source Serif 4 as the optional accent.
3. Logo: upload the logo file (PNG with transparent background, plus a white version for navy backgrounds).
4. Brand Voice and Brand Guidelines (if available on your plan): paste sections 1, 7 and 8.
