# Elevate & Thrive and Medical Sales Intelligence Hub: Brand Guidelines (for Canva)

Sources: your three brand images (Elevate & Thrive banner, Company Intelligence banner, square logo) and the Hub pages in this repo.
Hex values from the images were sampled from the artwork. Gold is a gradient, so three tones are given. Official brand hex values: Not verified.
CMYK values are screen-to-CMYK maths, not a print profile. Give the hex to your printer for a proper match.

## 1. Brand essence

* Brands: Elevate & Thrive (training and commercial intelligence) and the Medical Sales Intelligence Hub (the Hub, product).
* Audience: medical sales professionals and people moving into medical sales.
* Personality: premium, credible, calm, practical. Expert without being stiff.

### Taglines and descriptors (as shown on your artwork)

* Elevate & Thrive descriptor: Medical Sales Training & UK Medical Sales Commercial Intelligence
* Strapline: Practical training. Smarter intelligence. Stronger results.
* Hub: An analyst in your pocket
* Company Intelligence: Insight. Strategy. Advantage.
* Footer trio: Equipping medical sales professionals | Powering better conversations | Driving better outcomes
* Proof points: UK focused, Trusted data, Actionable insight, Confidential & compliant

### Inconsistencies to fix

* The square logo says "NHS Commercial Intelligence". The banner says "UK Medical Sales Commercial Intelligence". Pick one.
* The banner footer reads "medsalesintelligence hub". The domain is medsalesintelligencehub.co.uk.

### Usage ratio

* Navy and cream carry most layouts: roughly 70%.
* Gold is the accent: roughly 20%. Use it for headings, icons, rules and key words.
* Gold can be a gradient (dark to light) on the logo, arrows and large headings. Flat mid gold everywhere else.

## 3. Colour pairings

* Navy background: white text, gold headings and icons.
* Cream background: navy or near-black body text, gold headings, dark gold (#A07028) for small text.
* Do not use light gold text on cream. Contrast is too low.

## 4. Typography

Exact typefaces were not supplied. These are the closest matches to the artwork. Not verified.

* Wordmark and main headings (Elevate & Thrive): elegant high-contrast serif in capitals with wide letter-spacing. In Canva, try Cormorant Garamond or Trajan Pro style fonts.
* Descriptors and straplines: clean sans-serif capitals with wide letter-spacing.
* Banner headlines (Company Intelligence): bold sans-serif capitals, white and gold. Inter ExtraBold or Roboto Condensed Bold.
* Body: Inter Regular (also used on the Hub site), 1.6 to 1.75 line height.
* Editorial accent: Source Serif 4 (used on the Hub site), sparingly.

## 5. Logo

* Mark: four ascending bars (pill, IV bag, heartbeat line, medical cross) rising to the right, with a sweeping gold arrow above. Bars step up in height; the tallest carries the cross.
* Lock-up: mark above, then ELEVATE & THRIVE in wide-tracked gold serif capitals, then descriptor in white sans capitals, then a short gold rule.
* Primary version: gold on deep navy (#03152A).
* Light version (banner): navy bars with gold arrow and gold wordmark on cream.
* Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the tallest bar's cross. Minimum width and misuse rules: To confirm.
* Never place the gold logo on cream or photographs without the navy panel.
* Provide: gold-on-navy PNG, navy-on-cream PNG, and a transparent version for Canva.

## 6. Imagery and graphics

* Navy-and-gold only. Gold line icons (circle outline or plain) for features.
* Dark UK scenes and maps with gold glowing nodes for Company Intelligence.
* Laptop and phone mockups showing the Rep's Briefing for the Hub.
* Gold sweeping curve dividing cream and navy panels on banners.
* Real UK clinical and commercial settings. Nobody wears a tie in UK hospital images.
* Charts use gold, light gold, stone and slate blue-grey on navy.

## 7. Layout

* Generous white space. Thin gold rules rather than heavy boxes.
* Banner structure: logo and name left, three-column service icons below, contact bar on navy at the bottom, tagline strip on warm stone.
* Rounded corners about 8px on buttons and cards.
* Primary button: gold fill, navy text (as on the mockups). Secondary: outline.

3. Logo: upload the three logo versions listed in section 5.
4. Brand voice (if available on your plan): paste sections 1, 8 and 9.
