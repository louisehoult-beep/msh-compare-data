Reusable Two-Piece Blood Pressure Cuffs

Share

These comfortable, stain-resistant, latex-free cuffs are pre-fitted with connectors to fit any type of NIBP device. Available with single or double 50cm tubing and supplied with hook and loop fastenings.

Our NIBP Connector Configurations

Screw (Part Nos with SC08 Suffix)

Bayonet (with ST with SC12 Suffix)

Female Luer Slip (Part Nos with SC03 Suffix)

Male Locking (Part Nos with SC17 Suffix)

Female Locking (Part Nos with SC18 Suffix)

Double Screw (Part Nos with DC0808 Suffix)

Male, Female Locking (Part Nos with DC1718 Suffix)

Double Female Locking (Part Nos with DC1818 Suffix)
