Who it is for

Nurses who work in industry, and the companies that employ them

Nurses in industry sit between the clinic and the company. The work is different from ward or community nursing, and the profession has not always had a place of its own for it. This association is intended to be that place.

Clinical advisors and specialists

Nurses who support products and services in clinical settings and advise on how they are used.

Product and training roles

Product managers, product specialists and trainers who bring clinical practice into how products are built, explained and taught.

Employers

Companies that employ nurses can register too, and tell us how many nurses they employ, so the association reflects the whole sector.

Write a paper or an article

Have something to say about how nurses work in industry? Members can offer a paper or an article to be shared with the association. Tick the box on the form and tell us your topic.
